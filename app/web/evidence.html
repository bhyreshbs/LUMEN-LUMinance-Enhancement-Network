Findings

    Limits, ethics and licences

    • The main test set has 15 photos from one camera setup; another 100 come from a second public set. No claim is made about other cameras.
    • Test photos were never used for tuning. 91 photos of the second public set duplicate the main model's training photos, so that set is scored only with the model trained without them.
    • Restorations can change colour and fine detail. They are not forensic evidence.
    • The model weights and training data come from third parties; their licences and open questions are listed in the project's third-party notices.
