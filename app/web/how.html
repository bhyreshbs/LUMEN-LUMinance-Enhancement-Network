How LUMEN works

A photo taken in low light still holds most of its detail; it is just too dark to see. LUMEN finds that detail and brings the light back up, without inventing what was not there.

What happens to your photo

  1. You choose a photoIt is sent to the LUMEN server and kept in a temporary file while it is restored.
  2. Read and checkedFormat, size and integrity are checked. Photos larger than 768 px on the longest side are scaled down first.
  3. The light is measuredAverage brightness, how unevenly the light falls, and how much grain there is.
  4. RestoredThe style you chose brings back brightness, colour and detail.
  5. Bright areas checkedWindows and lamps keep their colour; Highlight-safe also softens clipped areas and grain where needed.
  6. Back to youThe result comes straight back to your browser and the temporary file is deleted.

While it works, the workspace shows each of these steps as it really finishes, with the time it took.

The three styles

Natural

A neural network restores light, colour and detail in one pass. The most faithful choice for most photos.

Retinexformer (ICCV 2023), official LOL-v1 weights, plus a highlight colour fix for near-clipped areas.

Highlight-safe

The same network, then targeted fixes only where its output still needs them: clipped lamps, windows and grain. If nothing needs fixing, the result is identical to Natural.

Quality-Guided Hybrid: feathered masks, highlight roll-off and guided-filter denoising, thresholds calibrated on a separate development split.

Quick

Classic image processing, no neural network. Fast and light, but less accurate on very dark photos.

Illumination estimate, Retinex brightening, illumination-aware denoising, CLAHE and colour correction, adapted to each photo.

What is and is not stored

  • Your photo is not kept. The upload is deleted as soon as the request ends, also when it fails.
  • The result is not kept. It is sent back in the response and never written to disk.
  • In your browser only: the technical details of your last restoration (cleared when you close the tab), and your theme choice.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. The hosting provider keeps standard request logs (time, address, page), not photos.

Limits

  • Very grainy photos. On photos far grainier than the test sets, Natural is not clearly better than Quick; Highlight-safe does better there. See the evidence.
  • Small test set. The headline numbers come from 15 test photos from one camera setup, so they are a guide, not a guarantee.
  • Not forensic. Restoration and denoising can change colour and fine detail; do not use results as evidence.
  • Size. Photos are restored at up to 768 px on the longest side.
