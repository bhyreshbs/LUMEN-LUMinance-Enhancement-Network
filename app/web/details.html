How it works

Each style is a different restoration method. All of them run on this server; nothing is sent anywhere else.

Natural

A deep neural network restores light, colour and detail in one pass.

Retinexformer (ICCV 2023), a Retinex-based transformer with 1.6 M parameters, using the official LOL-v1 weights.

Highlight-safe

The same network, then targeted fixes only where its output still needs them, such as clipped lamps or noisy patches. If nothing needs fixing, the result is identical to Natural.

Quality-Guided Hybrid: feathered masks; highlight roll-off and guided-filter denoising; thresholds calibrated on a separate development split.

Quick

Classic image processing with no neural network. Fast and lightweight, but less accurate on very dark photos.

Illumination estimation, Retinex brightening, illumination-aware denoising, CLAHE and colour correction, with parameters adapted to each photo.

    Limitations

    • The evaluation uses 15 test images from one dataset (LOL-v1 eval15). Results on other cameras and scenes may differ.
    • Photos are restored at up to 1024 px on the longest side.
    • Restorations are not forensic evidence: denoising and enhancement can alter colour and fine detail.
    • The model weights and training datasets come from third parties. Their licences and the open questions about them are documented in the project repository.
